## 👋 About Me
 
I'm a marketing leader with more than five years of experience driving enterprise revenue and brand equity. I've hired and led marketing and sales teams, built and managed demand generation playbooks, and delivered a 50% revenue CAGR and a 509% increase in branded search. I specialize in turning consumer insights into integrated campaigns that grow brand equity and market share. My strengths include market research, brand strategy, integrated communications and budget management.

**What I do best**
 
- 🔍 **Market research & brand diagnosis** — finding out what customers actually need before spending a dollar on media
- 🎯 **Segmentation, targeting & positioning** — psychographic segmentation using Category Entry Points and Jobs-to-Be-Done
- 📊 **Objectives & budget planning** — tying marketing spend to measurable business outcomes
- 📣 **Integrated marketing communications** — one consistent message across paid, owned, and earned channels
- 📈 **Brand measurement** — tracking funnel metrics, brand perceptions, and revenue impact over time
